Frequently Asked Questions

What accommodations are there nearby?

Guests have a few options regarding accommodations. Florence is a 30-minute drive away from the wedding venue and has many hotels and Airbnbs. There are also Airbnb options closer to the wedding venue. If you would like recommendations or support finding something suitable, please let us know.

How do I get to the wedding venue?

Both the ceremony & reception will be taking place at Castello di Mugnana.

Address: 106, Via di Mugnana, 50027 Strada In Chianti FI, Italy

The venue is situated 15km south of Florence, which translates to a 30-minute car ride. People can fly into Rome and drive or take the train to Florence, or fly into Florence directly. If you plan on renting a car, please note that having an international drivers license makes renting much easier. If you would not like to rent a car or scooter to travel around during the wedding weekend, drivers can be hired from the Florence airport to take you to the castle or nearby accommodations; a taxi costs between €30-50.

Is there a dress code for the wedding?

We believe people have the most fun when they feel comfortable. Please wear what you want to our wedding ceremony and reception, whether that be a a sundress, gown, suit, or something else.

Please note that the weather in Tuscany in mid-September ranges from 27°C during the day to 15°C at night.

Is there anything happening on the days before or after the wedding on September 19th?

There will be a casual get-together at the castle on Sunday, September 20th from 1:00-6:00 pm. We hope that you can join us for this additional opportunity to spend time together.

For more information and to see other suggested activities, which you can do on your own time, please visit our activities webpage.

Do you have a wedding registry?

We appreciate the time and expense that celebrating our wedding in person with us entails, and your presence is gift enough. As such, we do not have a wedding registry, however there will be a place for cards to be left at the reception should you wish to write us a note.

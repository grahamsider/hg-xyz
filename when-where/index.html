Where

Castello di Mugnana

106, Via di Mugnana, 50027 Strada In Chianti FI, Italy

Castello di Mugnana is a 12th century castle located 15km south of Florence. Fun fact: the medieval castle is a historic site and the place where The Tuscan League was signed in 1198.

The ceremony and reception will both take place at the castle.
